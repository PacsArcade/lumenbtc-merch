Before anything sells

Prices are yours. The plan file carries no retail numbers. Until you set them the script marks every product with a placeholder at twice Printful's cost, so nothing invented reaches a storefront.

Stickers print on white vinyl. Every sticker here carries its own dark or orange field so white type survives; anything transparent would have printed white-on-white.
